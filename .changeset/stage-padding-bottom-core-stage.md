sommo-io fork: a `paddingBottom` stage setting (screen px, default 0) — extra room past the content's bottom only, on top of `padding`, for a panel that floats over the viewport's foot. The camera clamp travels that much further down and the scroll range grows with it, so the last page can scroll clear of the panel; content that fits rests in the part above it.
